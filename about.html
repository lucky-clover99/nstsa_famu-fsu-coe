About NSTSA

Learn about the Nuclear Science and Technology Student Association at the FAMU–FSU College of Engineering.

Our Mission & Goals

Bringing students together through shared interests in nuclear science, technology, industry, and research.

Our mission is to provide opportunities for students from all disciplines to express their interest in nuclear industry and research. We aim to achieve this through academic, professional, and community-focused activities while encouraging collaboration amongst students with a shared interest in nuclear industry and research.

The Nuclear Science and Technology Student Association (NSTSA) aims to host monthly meetings, provide an audience for industry speakers, participate in volunteering, and visit nuclear related sites for tours.
